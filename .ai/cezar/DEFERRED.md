# Cezar graph workflows: deferred work

The `*.yaml` files in `.ai/cezar/workflows/` (`issue-to-merge`, `idea-to-spec`, `spec-to-backlog`,
`pr-to-merge`, `maintain-to-intent`, `scheduled-scan`, `housekeeping-graph`) are Cezar 0.15.2 graph
workflows (`version: 2`) ported from `design4pro/network`. They sit beside the older step-form `.yml`
workflows and replace none of them. Items below are intentionally left out because their
prerequisites do not exist yet. Each row names the trigger that makes it actionable. When a trigger
fires, do the item in its own PR and delete the row.

| Item                              | Where                                                                                                          | Trigger                                                                                          | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overlap with the `.yml` workflows | All `*.yaml` files                                                                                             | Before any of them is started by a person, an automation or the Reconciler                       | `issue-to-merge` and `pr-to-merge` do what `implement-ticket` and `pr-autopilot` do, `idea-to-spec` and `spec-to-backlog` what `spec` and `spec-to-tickets` do, `housekeeping-graph` what `housekeeping` does. The Reconciler (ADR 0033) and `docs/agents/cezar-automations.json` start only the `.yml` ones. Decide per pair which one is current; never point a trigger at both, or one issue is started twice.                                                                                   |
