# Domain glossary

A glossary and nothing else. No implementation details, no file paths, no commands. Terms are added lazily, during grilling sessions, when they earn their place. Decisions with rationale belong in docs/adr/, not here.

- **Organization (tenant)** — the unit of isolation and billing. Every product row belongs to exactly one organization; a user acts inside one organization at a time.
- **Workspace console** — the administrative surface where an organization manages members, billing and settings.
- **Trip** — the central planning object: a titled journey owned by an organization, with members and guests. It may be dated (a start and an end) or not yet: an undated trip still has a run of numbered days.
- **Day** — one numbered day of a trip, the unit the itinerary timeline is built from. It carries a calendar date once the trip is dated; the run of days always numbers 1..n without gaps.
- **Trip copy** — a new trip, owned by whoever copied it, that reproduces the source's plan (days, places and their tags, notes, budget lines, packing lists and to-dos with nothing ticked) and never its record (reservations, stays, transports, expenses, files, conversations, share tokens) or its people. Dates and title are chosen at copy time, defaulting to the source's.
- **Favourite** — one person's bookmark on a trip they may read. It is private to that person, shows nothing to anyone else and grants nothing. _Avoid_: "starred", "pinned", a favourite trip as a property of the trip.
- **Archive** — the reversible shelving of a trip: an archived trip leaves the default lists and the reminder scan but keeps everything, including its lifecycle status, and restoring it brings that status back unchanged. Archiving is not a status. _Avoid_: "archived" as a trip status.
- **Place** — a location attached to a trip, created by hand, from search or from an import. Its point is what puts it on the map; a place whose point is not known yet is still a place, listed but unmarked.
- **Reservation** — a booked commitment (lodging, activity, table) with dates, status and optional documents. _Avoid_: "booking" for the record itself; a booking desk is the screen where reservations are read, not a second name for one.
- **Import job** — the reading of one confirmation file, from the moment it is dropped on a trip's bookings screen until its rows are confirmed or the reader dismisses it. It is working, then ready (rows to review), refused (the file could not be read) or stalled (the app stopped waiting while the reading may still finish), and the reader can leave and find it again from any screen. A row becomes a reservation only when the reader confirms it. _Avoid_: "upload" for the job; an upload is only its first step.
- **Transport** — one journey between two endpoints, of a single mode, placeable in a day. It may leave on one day and land on another. A transport with more than two stations is made of **legs**.
- **Leg** — the part of a transport between two consecutive stations, carrying its own service number, platform, seat and times. N stations make N−1 legs; a plain two-station journey is one leg. _Avoid_: "leg" for a whole transport, "segment".
- **Stay** — a lodging held across a run of consecutive nights, belonging to every day it spans rather than to one. It carries its own name, check-in window and check-out, so it exists whether or not the property is also a place. A booked stay is two things on purpose: the reservation is the commitment, the stay is the nights on the calendar. _Avoid_: "accommodation" as the name of the concept.
- **Stop** — a place put on a particular day at a position in that day's order, with its own visit times and the mode by which it is reached: the unit an itinerary day is built from. A place appears at most once on a day. A reservation may be linked to a stop, which is how a booking gains a position in the day rather than only a time. _Avoid_: "assignment" outside the schema, "stop" for a transport's station.
- **Day location** — the point a day is spent at, taken from the stay that covers it (the one beginning that day wins, then the latest beginning) and otherwise from the first of the day's stops whose place has a point. A place with only one of its two coordinates is not a point, so it never gives a day a location; a day with none has none and anything that depends on one simply does not apply to it.
- **Map hand-off** — a link from a stop, or from a day's placed stops in visiting order, into the map application the reader navigates with, named for the service it opens. It carries coordinates the product already holds and computes nothing; a stop without a point has none, and a day that carries fewer stops than it has placed says so. _Avoid_: "export", "share" and "route" for it (a route is the line the map draws; routing is a separate, bought capability).
- **Budget entry / expense** — a cost recorded against a trip, splittable between members, carrying its own currency with a frozen exchange rate.
- **Budget currency** — the currency every figure of a trip adds up in, chosen when the trip is made. While nothing on the trip carries a price it can be changed when editing the trip; once anything does, it cannot be changed there, because every figure would keep its number and change what it is in. _Avoid_: "accounting currency" and "trip currency" as names for it.
- **Settlement** — the computed "who owes whom" resolution of split expenses.
- **Pack** — a packing list attached to a trip; items are checkable per member.
- **Weather reading** — what is known about the weather of one dated day of a trip at the place it is spent, of one of three kinds: a _forecast_ (the provider's prediction, for today and the days just ahead), _recorded_ (what was measured on a day already past) or an _estimate_ (the long-term average for that day's month, for days beyond the forecast). An estimate is always marked as one, and a day with no reading shows none, and a day that should have one and does not is _unavailable_, which is a different answer and never a zero. A past day the provider has not published yet is _not yet recorded_: neither of those, and never filled in from a forecast. _Avoid_: "archive" and "climate" for the kinds.
- **Weather slot** — one of the four six-hour stretches of a forecast day (night 00:00, morning 06:00, afternoon 12:00, evening 18:00, in the trip's own time), with the sky and the mean temperature over it. Six hours because that is the finest step the provider carries for every forecast day, so every day has the same four; a slot nobody has a forecast for - the hours of today already gone - is left out, never drawn empty. _Avoid_: "hourly strip", "column".
- **Sun times** — the sunrise and the sunset of one dated day of a trip at the place it is spent, as clock times in the trip's own time zone. They are computed from the place and the date, never fetched, so they cost no request and no allowance and every weather reading that has a fuller panel carries them. A side the sun does not cross the horizon for has no time: a whole day of polar night or midnight sun has neither, and on the day a polar period begins or ends only one of them is missing, and the panel says so by name instead of showing one. A trip with no time zone of its own shows no sun times, because they would be in UTC, not the place's clock. A side is the crossing on the day's own local calendar date, so where a sunset first slips past midnight (Reykjavik around 15 June) that one date shows no sunset. The trip has one time zone, so on a trip that crosses zones the far leg's times are out by the offset. _Avoid_: "daylight hours", "sun schedule".
- **Product module** — an optional, separately-toggled product area: Atlas (world-map tracker), Journal, Leave planner, Collections.
- **Atlas** — the world-map tracker module: countries and regions a user has visited.
- **Share token** — an unguessable token granting session-less, read-only access to a named projection of one trip resource.
- **Entitlement** — a capability granted by the organization's billing plan.
- **Quota** — a numeric ceiling on an entitlement, enforced at the data-access boundary.
- **Platform actor** — a privileged operator acting across organizations; every such action is audited.
- **Trip member** — a person with an account who is on a trip, holding one trip role: _owner_ (the trip's metadata, lifecycle and people; several are allowed), _editor_ (the plan's content and the trip's costs: expenses, their splits, payments and planned budget lines), _viewer_ (reading only, costs included). Every member may copy, favourite and leave. Membership decides who sees a private trip, who is reminded before it, and who can be assigned to its costs, items and plans. Distinct from an organization member, who merely belongs to the workspace.
- **Trip visibility** — who inside the organization may see a trip: _private_ (its members only, the default), _organization_ (every organization member), _public_ (reserved for session-less sharing). A trip a person may not see is indistinguishable to them from one that does not exist.
- **Guest** — a trip participant who is not an organization member: a display name only, unique within the trip, never an account, never a recipient. A guest can be merged into a trip member, who then inherits everything that was assigned to the guest; the guest ceases to exist.
- **Reminder** — a pre-departure notice delivered to a trip's members ahead of the start date, honouring each member's channel preferences; its lead time is counted in days, and zero means off.
- **System event** — a notification event with no acting user (a reminder, a billing follow-up); nobody is excluded from its recipients.
- **Entity history** — the per-entity view of a tenant's audit trail.
- **Orphaned journey** — a journey whose trip was deleted: it is kept and readable by its owner only. If its owner leaves the workspace it is unreadable by everyone and stops counting against the journeys quota; it counts again if they return. A journey that never had a trip is not an orphan and stays readable by the whole organization.
- **Erasure** — the removal, anonymisation or documented keeping of everything a person left, on request, across every workspace they ever took part in. It is more than deleting the account, which only ends the sign-in. _Avoid_: "account deletion" for it, and "departure" (leaving a workspace takes only the membership).
- **Former member** — the placeholder that stands for an erased person wherever their contribution to a shared record stays. One per workspace per erasure; it carries no name and cannot be traced back to the person. _Avoid_: "deleted user", "unknown user".
- **Stranded trip** — a trip whose visibility is not _organization_ and whose every trip member has left the workspace: nobody can read or delete it, so it is kept but stops counting against the trips quota and the plan-usage figure; it counts again if one of its members returns. A trip with _organization_ visibility is never stranded. _Avoid_: "orphaned trip".
- **Passkey** — a credential held by a person's device, password manager or security key that signs them in without a password. It is added and removed by the person themselves, never on their behalf by someone acting as them. _Avoid_: "security key" for the credential itself, "fingerprint".
